## Repeating patterns (n=25)
1. Name/greeting as H1 on personal sites: 9/10 (marclou the exception, leads with a result).
2. Client logo wall/strip on home: 10/25; inside or directly under the hero in 6.
3. Case studies or named work on home: 12/25.
4. Testimonials on home: 8/25; 0/6 of the AI-MVP shops.
5. Numeric pricing on home: 5/25; 0/10 established agencies beyond Parallel's "$10k" in FAQ.
6. Pricing as tier cards: 2/25 (HouseofMVPs, Designjoy); text "from $X": 3/25.
7. Process section on home: 6/6 productized/AI-MVP shops, 0/10 agencies.
8. Last nav item is a contact CTA: 9/25; Designjoy uses "See pricing".
9. Subscribe is the main CTA on personal sites: 5/10.
10. Fixed price + code ownership stated as promises: 4/25 (all AI-MVP shops).
11. Speed claim in headline/title: 3/25.
12. Text-only hero: ~10/25. Real photo of the person in the hero: 4/25 (marclou, Stark, Comeau, swyx).
13. Zero hero CTAs: 9/25 (mostly personal sites); one primary CTA: ~6/25.
14. Segmented-intent hero (visitor picks a path): 2/25 (HouseofMVPs, BeevR).

## Standouts
bruno-simon.com (site is a game), marclou.com (revenue scoreboard incl. failures), jonathanstark.com
(one crusade drives everything), singhlabs.dev (voice + own product), rauno.me (manifesto replaces sections).
