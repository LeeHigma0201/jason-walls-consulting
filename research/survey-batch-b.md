## Repeating patterns (n=24 readable)
1. Hero primary CTA is "book a call / demo": 11/24; sign-up or trial: 9/24.
2. Last nav item is a contact CTA: 10/24; sign-up: 5/24.
3. Named-person testimonials: 18/24.
4. Quantified case studies or outcome stats on home: 11/24.
5. Client logo strip: 11/24; partner/Clutch badges instead: 6/24.
6. Pricing on the home page: 6/24 (full numbers: Hook, Raft, Orbix). Public /pricing numbers: 12 of 14 fetched.
7. Pricing layout on public pricing pages: cards 7, tables 4, "from $X" headline 1.
8. Explicit numbered process on home: 5/24 (3–5 steps); numbered pillars instead: 5/24; none: 14/24.
9. Industry/trade picker in nav or hero: 8/24.
10. Hero visual: text-only 5/24; product screenshots 4/24; real people photos 4/24 (Hook, Rocket, Jobber, Superhuman).
11. Agent/API surfaces named on home (MCP, .md, CLI): 7/24.
12. Trade agencies: "Home Services" in headline 4/5; "you own 100% of assets" 3/5; no pricing 4/5; 6–8 nav items with mega dropdowns 5/5.

## Standouts
37signals.com (point of view is the site), uxcontinuum.com (one-engineer voice + full priced menu),
raftlabs.com (price + week-by-week timeline in hero, pre-qualifies buyers), hookagency.com (only trade
agency with published prices, founder pledge), raycast.com (manifesto + named-figure testimonials, no logo wall).
